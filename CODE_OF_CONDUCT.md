# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make participation in this project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing disagreements.
- Provide actionable feedback rather than personal attacks.
- Respect differing experience levels.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Repeated disruptive behavior after a request to stop.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact method listed on the GitHub profile. Include enough context to understand the concern, but avoid sharing unnecessary personal information.

The maintainer may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project respectful.
